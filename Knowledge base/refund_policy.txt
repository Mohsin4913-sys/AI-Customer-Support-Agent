Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of receiving their order.

The product must be unused and returned in its original condition.

Customers are responsible for returning the product unless the product was damaged or defective when received.

Once the returned product is received and inspected, eligible refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Items purchased during clearance sales are not eligible for refunds unless they are defective.
