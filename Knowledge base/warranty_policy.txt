Warranty Policy

Products are covered by a one-year limited warranty from the date of purchase.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures that occur under normal use.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modifications, or normal wear and tear.

Customers must provide proof of purchase when submitting a warranty claim.

If a product is determined to have a covered manufacturing defect, the company may repair or replace the product according to the warranty terms.
